I. Introduction & Theoretical Motivation

Foundational premise, causal puzzle, and the historical instrument hypothesis.
¶ 01
What are the fundamental causes of the large differences in income per capita across countries? While there is little doubt that physical capital, human capital, and technology are immediate determinants of economic prosperity, we argue that differences in institutional quality—specifically, the degree of property rights enforcement and protection against expropriation risk—are the primary fundamental cause of comparative development. However, estimating the impact of institutions on economic performance is notoriously difficult because economies with high GDP per capita may simply afford better institutions, generating acute simultaneity bias and reverse causality. To overcome this fundamental identification challenge, we propose a novel theory exploiting historical differences in European settler mortality rates as an exogenous instrument for contemporary institutional quality across former colonies.
中文学术意译 (Top Journal Style)
究竟何为导致跨国人均收入巨大差距的根本动因？尽管物质资本、人力资本与技术进步无疑是经济繁荣的直接决定因素，但本文主张，制度质量的异质性——特别是产权界定清晰度与防范政府掠夺风险的保护机制——构成了决定比较发展差异的根本原因。然而，评估制度对经济绩效的因果影响面临显著的识别困境：富裕国家往往有能力负担更为完善的制度设计，从而引发严重的同步性偏差与反向因果难题。为破解这一内生性瓶颈，本文提出一种基于历史维度的全新理论，将欧洲早期殖民者在外派定居过程中的死亡率差异，作为测度当代殖民地制度质量的外生工具变量。

¶ 02
Our causal chain rests on three core institutional linkages. First, in environments where Europeans faced relatively low mortality risks (such as the United States, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand), they migrated in large numbers and created 'neo-Europes' characterized by inclusive institutions that placed checks on government power and enforced the rule of law. Conversely, in regions with hostile disease environments where settlement was infeasible (such as the Congo, Nigeria, or the Caribbean), Europeans established 'extractive states' whose main objective was to transfer resources to the metropole without introducing civil liberties or property protections. Second, colonial institutional architectures exhibit formidable institutional persistence, lasting well beyond decolonization into the modern era because post-independence elites inherited and repurposed extractive machinery to enrich themselves. Consequently, historical settler mortality influences current economic prosperity almost exclusively through its persistent imprint on institutional development.
中文学术意译 (Top Journal Style)
本文所建构的因果链条立足于三重相互锁定的制度传导机制。首先，在早期欧洲殖民者面临极低死亡风险的温带定居区（如美国、加拿大、澳大利亚与新西兰），欧洲人实现了大规模移民拓殖，建立了以制约行政权力与保障法治为特征的‘新欧洲型包容性制度’；相反，在疟疾与黄热病肆虐的高危疾病环境区（如刚果、尼日利亚或加勒比群岛），定居根本无法实现，殖民者转而构建了‘掠夺型制度’，其唯一诉求在于将原产地自然资源最大限度榨取并转移至宗主国，而绝不赋予当地社会以财产权与公民自由。其次，殖民时期的制度架构具有极其坚韧的路径依赖特征，在非殖民化浪潮后依然长期存续，原因在于独立后的本土官僚精英直接继承了这套掠夺机器并用以攫取租金。由此，历史上的定居者死亡率唯有通过其在制度演化中的历史沉淀，方能持续投影于当代的经济繁荣。

II. Econometric Methodology & Empirical Results

Two-stage least squares (2SLS) identification and structural estimation.
¶ 03
To estimate the causal effect of institutions on economic performance, we formulate the structural econometric relationship using two-stage least squares (2SLS). The second-stage equation relates current income per capita to institutional protection: $$\log y_i = \mu + \alpha R_i + \mathbf{X}_i' \gamma + \varepsilon_i$$ where $y_i$ is GDP per capita in 1995 for country $i$, $R_i$ is contemporary institutional quality measured by the Political Risk Services index of protection against expropriation risk between 1985 and 1995, and $\mathbf{X}_i$ is a vector of covariates including continent dummies, absolute latitude, and legal origins. In the first-stage regression, we instrument current institutions using the logarithm of historical European settler mortality: $$R_i = \zeta + \beta \log M_i + \mathbf{X}_i' \delta + v_i$$ where $M_i$ represents settler mortality rates per 1,000 per annum based on nineteenth-century military records. For $\log M_i$ to serve as a valid instrumental variable, it must satisfy both relevance ($\beta \neq 0$) and the critical exclusion restriction ($E[\log M_i \cdot \varepsilon_i] = 0$), meaning mortality had no direct impact on modern prosperity other than through institutional persistence.
中文学术意译 (Top Journal Style)
为精确测度制度对经济绩效的净因果效应，本文采用两阶段最小二乘法（2SLS）构建结构计量模型。第二阶段方程将当代人均收入与产权制度保护程度建立如下映射：$$\log y_i = \mu + \alpha R_i + \mathbf{X}_i' \gamma + \varepsilon_i$$ 其中 $y_i$ 表示样本国 $i$ 在 1995 年的对数人均实际国内生产总值；$R_i$ 为当代制度质量的代理指标，具体采用政治风险服务集团（PRS）评估的 1985 至 1995 年间‘防范政府征收风险指数’；$\mathbf{X}_i$ 为一组基准控制变量向量，涵盖洲际虚拟变量、绝对纬度以及法系渊源。在第一阶段回归中，本文引入 19 世纪欧洲早期定居者死亡率对数 $\log M_i$ 作为核心工具变量：$$R_i = \zeta + \beta \log M_i + \mathbf{X}_i' \delta + v_i$$ 该历史死亡率数据来源于驻防欧洲陆军与海军官兵每千人年化死亡记录。为确保该工具变量在计量上严格成立，$\log M_i$ 必须同时满足相关性假设（即 $\beta \neq 0$）与至关重要的排他性约束条件（$E[\log M_i \cdot \varepsilon_i] = 0$），即殖民者死亡率除通过塑造制度传承外，不得对当代经济产出产生任何未被控制的直接渠道影响。

¶ 04
The 2SLS estimates uncover a strikingly powerful causal impact of institutions. In the first-stage regression, historical settler mortality is strongly and negatively correlated with modern institutional quality: $\hat{\beta} = -0.61$ with a standard error of $0.13$ ($t = -4.69$, $F > 20$), ruling out weak-instrument concerns. In the structural second-stage, the estimated coefficient on protection against expropriation risk is $\hat{\alpha} = 0.94$ (standard error $0.16$, $p < 0.001$), indicating that a one-standard-deviation improvement in institutional security multiplies income per capita by nearly fourfold. When controlling for latitude, temperature, and malaria ecology directly in the second stage, the coefficient $\hat{\alpha}$ remains quantitatively stable and statistically indistinguishable from $0.94$, whereas geographical covariates lose explanatory power. Furthermore, controlling for British colonial heritage and religion does not attenuate this coefficient, confirming that our findings are not artifacts of omitted cultural or imperial traditions.
